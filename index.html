About Northern Point

Northern Point Accountants is an accounting practice based in Chatswood. We work with individuals and business owners on the everyday work of keeping records and meeting lodgment deadlines, and on bigger decisions such as choosing a business structure or planning for a change in cash flow. We explain what the numbers mean so you can decide what to do next.
